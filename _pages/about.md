## About
I am a Ph.D. candidate in Economics at the Guanghua School of Management, Peking University. 

My research lies at the intersection of economic history and political economy. I am particularly interested in how states interact with elites in historical settings, and how these interactions shape political institutions, state capacity, social order, and long-run development. My current research studies topics including state formation, elite co-optation, and the political economy of bureaucratic institutions.
